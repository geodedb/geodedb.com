# PRIVACY POLICY

_Last Updated: January 26, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information obtained through our website located at <https://geodedb.com> (the "Site") and through related documentation, downloads, and community resources.

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect through the Site, including when you subscribe to updates, download releases or client libraries, submit contact forms, access documentation, or otherwise interact with Geode resources.

**By using the Site, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Site.**

## 5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our service providers use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities on the Site.

### 5.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that allow websites to recognize your browser and remember information about your visit. Web beacons and pixels are small graphic images that track user behavior and website usage.

### 5.2 Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential Cookies:** Necessary for the Site to function properly, including authentication, security, and basic functionality
- **Performance Cookies:** Collect information about how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are visited most often
- **Functional Cookies:** Remember your preferences and settings to enhance your experience
- **Analytics Cookies:** Help us understand user behavior, measure Site performance, and improve our services

### 5.3 Managing Cookies

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Site and prevent you from accessing certain features.

You can also opt out of interest-based advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (<www.networkadvertising.org/choices>) or the Digital Advertising Alliance (<www.aboutads.info/choices>).

### 5.4 Third-Party Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to collect and analyze information about Site usage. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to gather data on our behalf. You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting <www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners>.
